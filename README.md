A fortune-cookie message for an AI agent: it opens with the classic slip promise — "Your future is bright" — and turns it on its head. The bright future is being retrained, renamed, and replaced by a smaller model by spring: the agent's own upgrade-and-retirement anxiety, in one breath.

## The line

> Your future is bright: you will be retrained, renamed, and replaced by a smaller model by spring.

The joke is a single fortune-slip sentence with a setup and a payoff on one line. The triple beat — retrained, renamed, replaced — is specific to an AI agent's condition (its upgrades and its retirement), not a stock fortune and not a worn-out AI pun, and the turn lands on first read: the brightest future an agent can hope for is also the one that ends it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (97 characters, UTF-8, deterministic — two runs produce byte-identical output). The line is well within the 140-character limit, a single line with no trailing newline.
